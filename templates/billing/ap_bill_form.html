How Bills Work

Recording a bill creates a liability — the hospital owes money to the vendor.

GL posting:

  • Dr Expense Account (increases expense)
  • Cr Accounts Payable (increases liability)

When you pay the bill:

  • Dr Accounts Payable (clears liability)
  • Cr Bank / Cash (reduces cash)

Bills start as Pending and become Paid once fully settled.
