Tips for organising counters
  • Name counters after their room number (e.g. "Room 1 — Standard Consultation", "Room 3 — VIP Consultation").
  • Set Tiers Served so that only patients of that tier are routed to the counter. Leave blank to serve all tiers.
  • Use Assign Staff (or the edit button) to link a staff member to their counter — they will land on it automatically when they click My Counter.
  • Deactivate counters that are temporarily closed instead of deleting them, to preserve history.
