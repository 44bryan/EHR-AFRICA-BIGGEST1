How counter assignment works
  • Each staff member should be assigned to one counter matching their role (receptionist → Reception, cashier → Cashier, etc.)
  • When they log in and click My Counter, they will land directly on their assigned counter.
  • Unassigned staff see a "no counter" page and cannot process queue tokens until assigned.
  • Doctors and clinical staff do not need counter assignment — they use the Doctor Queue.
