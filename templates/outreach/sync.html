{# ── Field App Instructions ─────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
Field App Setup Instructions
1
Before leaving
Copy the outreach_field/ folder to the outreach laptop. Run run_field.bat (Windows) or run_field.sh (Linux/Mac) to start the local server at http://localhost:8765
2
In the field
Open http://localhost:8765 in any browser. Register patients, record examinations, generate referral letters. All data is saved locally — no internet needed.
3
Back at hospital
Connect to hospital network, then click the Sync Now button above. All field records upload automatically. The result shows here immediately.
