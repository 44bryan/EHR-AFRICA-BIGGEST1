General Pre-Operative Instructions

  • Fasting: Do not eat or drink anything (including water) for at least 6–8 hours before your surgery time.
  • Medications: Continue your routine medications unless specifically told otherwise by your doctor. Bring a list of all your current medications.
  • Eye drops: If prescribed pre-op drops, use them exactly as instructed the morning of surgery.
  • Arrival: Arrive at the hospital at least 1 hour before your scheduled surgery time for pre-op preparation.
  • Clothing: Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing. Avoid wearing makeup, perfume, or jewellery.
  • Escort: You must come with a responsible adult who will accompany you home. Do not drive yourself to the hospital.
  • Glasses/Contact Lenses: Do not wear contact lenses on the day of surgery. Bring your glasses.
  • Documents: Bring your patient card, any prior test results, and this instruction sheet.
⚠ Important: Contact us immediately at {{ HOSPITAL_PHONE }} if you develop fever, eye pain, increased redness, or any new symptoms before your surgery date.

Post-Operative Care (after surgery)

  • Use prescribed eye drops exactly as directed — do not skip doses.
  • Wear your eye shield / patch as instructed, especially when sleeping.
  • Do not rub or press on the operated eye.
  • Avoid swimming, dusty environments, and strenuous physical activity for at least 2 weeks.
  • Do not bend your head below waist level for the first week.
  • Attend all follow-up appointments as scheduled.
