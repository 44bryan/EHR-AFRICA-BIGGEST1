Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. 1
    Patient Arrives — Insurance Card Identified
    Gate Staff
    System screen: QMS → Gate Issue
    • Gate staff identifies patient as insured when they present their insurance/company card
    • Issue QMS gate ticket — select patient type: Insured
    • Direct patient to Reception / Insurance Verification Desk
  2. 2
    Insurance Verification at Reception
    Receptionist / Medical Records
    System screen: Patients → Detail → Insurance Tab
    • Ask patient for: Insurance card, Company ID, Authorization letter (if required)
    • Retrieve or register patient in system
    • Open patient record → Insurance tab
    • Verify: Insurance company name, policy number, coverage type, expiry date
    • Confirm coverage includes ophthalmology / eye care services
    • Note: Does the insurance require Pre-Authorization before procedures?
    • Create or update insurance record in system
  3. 3
    Triage — Vitals & Chief Complaint
    OPD Nurse
    System screen: Consultations → Triage Workstation
    Same as standard flow:
    • Record VA (right and left eye), IOP, blood pressure, chief complaint
    • Note any allergies
    • Submit triage — patient enters normal doctor queue
  4. 4
    Doctor Consultation
    Ophthalmologist / Doctor
    System screen: Consultations → Workstation
    • Consultation follows standard clinical workflow
    • Doctor should be aware the patient is insured — avoid ordering uncovered services without warning patient
    • If surgery or costly procedure is recommended, note: Pre-Authorization Required
    • Complete diagnosis, prescriptions, lab orders, investigation orders
    • Click Complete Consultation
  5. 5
    Pre-Authorization (If Required by Insurer)
    Medical Records / Billing Officer
    System screen: Billing → Proforma or Corporate Accounts
    • For procedures (surgery, investigations, expensive drugs): call or email insurer for pre-auth
    • Record pre-authorization number in the system on the patient's invoice/proforma
    • Once approved, proceed to billing
    • If not required: skip directly to billing step
  6. 6
    Billing — Proforma / Insurance Claim Creation
    Cashier / Billing Officer
    System screen: Billing → Proforma or Billing → Cashier POS
    • Open patient billing screen — all charges auto-populated from consultation
    • Create a Proforma Invoice addressed to the insurance company / corporate employer
    • Set billing tier: Insurance / Corporate Rate (if service price tiers are configured)
    • Split bill:
      • Insurance portion → billed to company/insurer on credit
      • Co-payment portion → patient pays now
    ItemTotalInsurance CoversPatient Co-pay
    Consultation—As per policyBalance / fixed co-pay
    Medications—Listed drugs onlyNon-covered drugs
    Lab / Imaging—If pre-authorizedUncovered tests
  7. 7
    Co-Payment at Cashier
    Cashier
    System screen: Billing → Cashier POS
    • Patient pays only the co-payment amount (not the full bill)
    • Process co-pay payment → receipt issued to patient
    • Insurance portion stays as outstanding on the corporate account, to be claimed later
    • Pharmacy release is triggered when co-pay is marked PAID
  8. 8
    Pharmacy Dispensing
    Pharmacist
    System screen: Pharmacy → Dispensing Queue
    • Prescription appears in queue after co-pay is confirmed
    • Dispense insurance-covered medications from system stock
    • Non-covered medications: patient pays full price separately
    • Mark as dispensed
  9. 9
    Patient Exit & Claim Filing
    Billing / Medical Records
    • Patient exits with medication and co-pay receipt
    • Billing officer consolidates claim at end of day/week and submits to insurer
    • Track reimbursements in Billing → Corporate Accounts → Statement
Dry Run Watch Points: (1) Insurance information must be recorded in patient record before consultation starts. (2) Proforma must reference the correct corporate account. (3) Do not release medication until co-pay is confirmed paid — even for insured patients.
Coverage Tip: If you are unsure whether a service is covered by a specific insurer, always ask the patient to confirm with their employer/insurer before ordering the service. Document this conversation in the consultation notes.
