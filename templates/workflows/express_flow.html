Express Patient Flow

Pre-pay model · Skip triage queue · Shortest possible waiting time
Flow TypeExpress Avg Duration15 – 35 min Starts AtExpress Cashier Desk Ends AtPharmacy / Exit QMSExpress Priority Queue
Arrival› Express Desk (Pay First)› Express Ticket› Brief Triage› Priority Consult› Pharmacy› Exit
What is Express? A patient who does not want to wait in the standard queue can opt into the Express lane by paying the consultation fee upfront at the Express Desk. They then receive a priority queue ticket and are seen before standard-queue patients.

Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. 1
    Patient Arrives & Requests Express Service
    Gate Staff / Receptionist
    System screen: QMS → Gate Issue
    • Patient arrives and indicates they want the Express service
    • Gate staff directs them directly to the Express/Cashier Desk — skip standard registration queue
    • Returning patients already registered; new patients will be registered at the express desk
  2. 2
    Express Desk — Registration & Upfront Payment
    Cashier (Express Desk)
    System screen: QMS → Express Desk + Billing → Cashier POS
    • Search for existing patient or do quick registration if new
    • Create invoice for Consultation Fee (Express rate if applicable)
    • Collect payment upfront: Cash / Mobile Money / Card
    • Issue payment receipt
    • Open QMS → Express Desk and issue an Express Priority Ticket
    • Ticket shows EXPRESS prefix — displayed on queue board with priority
  3. 3
    Brief Triage
    OPD Nurse
    System screen: Consultations → Triage Workstation
    • Express patients still require basic vitals — nurse sees them immediately, ahead of standard queue
    • Record VA (right eye, left eye), IOP, chief complaint
    • Takes 3–5 minutes maximum
    • Patient enters Express Doctor Queue after triage
  4. 4
    Priority Doctor Consultation
    Doctor
    System screen: Consultations → Workstation
    • Express tickets appear at the top of the doctor queue (marked EXPRESS)
    • Doctor calls the express patient next — before waiting standard patients
    • Perform consultation in the normal way
    • Issue prescriptions / additional orders as needed
    • Consultation complete
  5. 5
    Additional Billing (if required)
    Cashier
    System screen: Billing → Work Queue
    • Consultation fee was pre-paid at Step 2
    • If doctor added new orders (lab tests, investigations, medications), those appear as a new bill
    • Patient returns to cashier or Express Desk to pay for additional services
    • Then proceeds to pharmacy / lab
  6. 6
    Priority Pharmacy Dispensing
    Pharmacist
    System screen: Pharmacy → Dispensing Queue
    • Express prescriptions flagged in dispensing queue for priority service
    • Dispense medications — patient should not wait more than 5 minutes
    • Mark prescription as dispensed
  7. 7
    Patient Exit
    —
    Patient exits with medication and receipt. Follow-up appointment printed if needed. Entire visit should be completed within 35 minutes from arrival.
Staff Note: The Express lane is a paid priority upgrade — do not allow patients to join the express queue without completing payment at Step 2. Standard patients cannot be downgraded to make room for express patients beyond the standard queue rules.
Dry Run Target: From Express Desk payment to doctor consultation start should be under 15 minutes. Monitor queue board to ensure EXPRESS tickets are always called before STANDARD tickets of the same consultation unit.
