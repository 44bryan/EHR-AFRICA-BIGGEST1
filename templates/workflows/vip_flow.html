Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. 1
    VIP Patient Arrival
    VIP Receptionist / Security
    System screen: QMS → VIP Reception
    VIP patients use the dedicated VIP entrance. Security notifies VIP reception desk immediately.
    • No gate queue ticket issued — VIP patients skip standard gate process
    • VIP receptionist greets patient and escorts to VIP reception desk
    • Open QMS → VIP Reception to log the arrival and issue a VIP token
  2. 2
    VIP Check-In & Registration
    VIP Receptionist
    System screen: Patients → Search or Patients → Register
    • Returning VIP: Search and open existing record — confirm demographics
    • New VIP: Complete full registration with all details — VIP tag noted in record
    • Confirm appointment if pre-booked (check Appointments screen)
    • Notify the assigned doctor immediately by phone/intercom
    • Offer water, seating in VIP lounge while waiting (minimal wait expected)
  3. 3
    Nurse Assessment (Bedside / In Room)
    OPD Nurse
    System screen: Consultations → Triage Workstation
    • Nurse goes to VIP consultation room — takes vitals bedside (not at triage bay)
    • Record VA, IOP, blood pressure, chief complaint directly in the system
    • No queue — results go directly to the attending doctor's workstation
  4. 4
    VIP Consultation Room — Doctor
    Senior Ophthalmologist / Consultant
    System screen: Consultations → Workstation → VIP Room
    • Doctor opens patient record — triage vitals already populated
    • Select department: VIP Consultation Room
    • Perform full clinical examination
    • Add diagnoses, issue prescriptions, lab or investigation orders as needed
    • Spend additional time explaining findings to patient (VIP standard of care)
    • Optionally print Consultation Summary for patient to take home
    • Book follow-up if required
  5. 5
    Priority Billing — At VIP Desk or In Room
    VIP Receptionist / Cashier
    System screen: Billing → Cashier POS
    • VIP patients are NOT directed to the main cashier queue
    • Billing is handled at the VIP reception desk or a dedicated VIP cashier terminal
    • Invoice is pre-populated from the consultation orders
    • Accept: Cash, card, mobile money, or corporate account charge
    • Print official receipt — VIP header letterhead if applicable
  6. 6
    Priority Pharmacy
    Pharmacist
    System screen: Pharmacy → Dispensing Queue
    • Pharmacist is notified of VIP prescription — prioritise over regular queue
    • Medications pre-packaged and delivered to VIP lounge or reception desk
    • Counsel patient on dosage — written instructions printed if needed
  7. 7
    VIP Exit
    VIP Receptionist
    • Escort patient to exit or vehicle if required
    • Provide appointment reminder card for next visit
    • Record satisfaction feedback (optional) in Feedback module
VIP Service Standard: Maximum wait time between any step is 5 minutes. The assigned doctor and nurse should be notified as soon as the VIP patient arrives at reception — not when they are called.
Important: VIP patients must still be fully registered in the system. Do not skip registration to save time — the clinical record is required for patient safety and billing.
